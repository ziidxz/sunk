01 / The gap

A card statement tells you after the money leaves.

Subscriptions are small, predictable and easy to stop noticing. A trial becomes a charge. A yearly plan renews in a month you were not watching. A price goes up and the email lands in a folder you do not read.

Most banking apps are built to show what happened. Sunk is built around what is about to happen.

There is no bank connection to approve and no account to create. You type what you pay for; the page does the arithmetic.

The monthly cost, the yearly cost, the next charge, the price change and the trial date sit on one screen instead of six billing pages.

02 / The ledger

Four questions, answered before the charge.

01

What leaves every month

Every weekly, monthly and yearly plan becomes one monthly figure and one yearly figure, so a $120 renewal never reads like a $10 one.

02

What renews next

Overdue charges and the next 30 days sit at the top; trial endings are called out separately, because that is the renewal people miss.

03

What changed price

When you edit a price, the old one stays in the row's history with the date it started, so you see what the service costs now.

04

What cancelling gives back

Marking a row cancelled shows the yearly amount that stops leaving, and keeps the entry in the ledger.

03 / Privacy

Your bank never enters this page.

Sunk has no backend to send your ledger to. The web edition is a set of static files, and the entries are stored by your browser.

  • No bank login There is no account to link, no statement to upload, and no credential to hand over.
  • No analytics The app does not load a tracking script or set an advertising cookie.
  • Stored in this browser Subscriptions live in localStorage on your device. Clearing site data deletes them unless you keep a JSON backup.
  • Offline when you buy the file The offline edition does not need a network connection after you save it.

06 / Questions

The parts people ask about first.

Does Sunk connect to my bank?

No. There is no bank login, no statement upload and no credential; you type the subscriptions yourself. The paid edition can read a CSV from your bank in the browser without uploading it.

Where is my data stored?

In this browser, using localStorage; there is no server copy and no account. Export a JSON backup for safety, because clearing site data deletes the ledger.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. The web edition can be added to your home screen. The data stays in that browser, so a phone and a laptop do not sync; the offline file opens on a phone too.

Why is CSV import paid?

It is the part with the most edge cases and the part heavy users need. The free edition stays useful with manual entry and five subscriptions.

How fast does the file arrive?

Delivery is by email once payment clears, handled by hand. Allow up to two business days, then write again if nothing has arrived.

Who am I buying from?

One person, not a company. The maker reads the order email, sends the file and handles refunds at ziidcxz@gmail.com.

What if the paid file does not work?

Write back within 14 days and the payment is refunded. The details are on the order page and in the terms.
